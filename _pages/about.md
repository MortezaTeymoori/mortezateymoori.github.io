I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Microsystems for Biomedical Imaging Laboratory](https://www.imtek.uni-freiburg.de/) at IMTEK, University of Freiburg. I build photonic and RF systems for magnetic resonance imaging: optical links that carry both power and the MR signal over fibre, photonic integrated circuits that process those signals, and the receive-chain electronics that make fully optical MRI coil arrays possible. Our latest work, [**Light Coils**]({{ '/lightcoils/' | relative_url }}), demonstrates MRI with fully optical data and power transmission.

My background sits at the intersection of electromagnetics and photonics. I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Boğaziçi University (Istanbul) in 2024 with a thesis on *Metamaterials and Their Applications in Biomedical Engineering* (advised by Prof. Arda Deniz Yalçınkaya), which received the Necmi Tanyolaç Award for the best Ph.D. thesis. During that time I designed RF metasurfaces for 3T and 7T MRI, microwave and THz metamaterial sensors, and MEMS-on-fibre sensors, and worked as an R&D engineer at GlakoLens on a smart contact lens that went through first-in-human pilot studies.

I like to take projects end to end: from numerical design (CST, COMSOL, Lumerical) through cleanroom fabrication to experimental validation on the bench and in the scanner.

**Research interests:** photonic integrated circuits · RF photonics and power-over-fibre · MRI coil hardware · electromagnetic metamaterials · MEMS and microfabrication.
